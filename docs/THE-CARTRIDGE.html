The cartridge

The banks

128 KB in 16 banks of 8 KB with the Konami SCC mapper: four windows (0x4000, 0x6000, 0x8000 and 0xA000) switched by writing the bank number to 0x5000, 0x7000, 0x9000 and 0xB000. Bank 0x3F at 0x9000 opens the SCC at 0x9800-0x98FF.

Bank 0 always stays at 0x4000 and the other three windows are set three at a time: 1-2-3 (p00:438D), 7-8-9 (p00:4392), 10-11-12 (p00:4397) and 13-14-15 (p00:439F). The current set is kept at 0xDDF1-0xDDF3. The sound puts banks 4-5-6 in for a moment and goes back to the previous set.

bankswhat is there
p00the core: start-up, the interrupt, the state dispatcher (p00:4220), the VDP, pictures, texts and sound requests
p01-p03the game: pitching, batting, runners, fielding, camera, scoreboard and batting order
p04-p06the sound: the table of 72 sounds (p04:600C), the scores (p05) and the engine (p06)
p07-p12the graphics: palettes, pictures, the field map (p08:9432) and its tiles, the kanji (p09), the sprites (p10-p11) and the font (p12)
p13-p15the menus, PENNANT, TEAM EDIT, saving, the intro and the six teams (p13:731F)

The pictures

Almost everything on screen comes from picture lists (p00:4FA3): a number of entries and, per entry, 6 bytes [palette-type][VRAM][n][pointer]. The type says how many bits each pixel has: 0 and 1, one bit; 2 and 3, two; 4 and 5, three. Odd types are drawn mirrored. The high nibble picks the palette in a table that gives 2, 4 or 8 colours; each bit combination is one of them.

Large things go in runs (p00:4B35): a count byte; 0 ends, with bit 7 clear it repeats the next byte, 0x80 brings a new destination and 0x81 to 0xFF copy the bytes as they are.

The texts

p00:4EA6 and its sisters put banks 13-14-15 in and read [x y] and font characters up to 0xFF; 0xFE brings another position and 0xFD is a half space. See The texts.

The sound

Each sound has a header (p04:600A + 2 × number): a channel mask (bit 7 channel 1, bit 0 channel 8; 1 to 3 are the PSG, 4 to 8 the SCC), a priority and a pointer to each channel's score. In the scores, 0xD0 to 0xFE are commands (p06:B92F: the octave, the priority and others through a table), what is below are notes and 0xFF ends. The engine steps each channel in the interrupt (p06:B41E) and writes the PSG and the SCC (p06:BBB8). See The music.

Saving

TEAM EDIT and PENNANT save to one of three places: RAM, disk or the Game Master 2's SRAM (p13:6007).

  • Disk goes through the BDOS (0xF37D). Before each call the game's RAM that DOS would overwrite is moved aside (p14:8334) and put back after.
  • The Game Master 2 is searched for in the four slots and their subslots: it is the cartridge with YZ at 0x4010 (p13:709A). To use it, the game puts its bank 4 at 0x8000 and calls 0x8000 with the command in C: 5 the directory, 0x0A save and 0x0B load (p13:721D). The league is saved under the name PENAN. Meanwhile it removes the H.KEYI hook (H.TIMI for the directory).

The Pro Yakyuu Gekitotsu Pennant Race was published by Konami for the MSX2 in 1988; its catalogue number is RC-757 and it is 128 KB with the SCC. All rights remain with their holders. This is preservation, study and documentation work, and the cartridge image is not distributed.
