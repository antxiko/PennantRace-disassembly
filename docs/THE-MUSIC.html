The music

26 pieces. The sound engine (bank 6, with the scores in bank 5 and the table of 72 sounds at p04:600C) drives eight channels: the PSG's three and the SCC's five. Each sound's header says which channels it uses: the tunes use 6 or 8 and the effects only two, PSG channel 3 and SCC channel 1. A channel takes a new sound if its priority is not lower than that of the one playing (p06:B305).

During a game the background tune depends on the furthest occupied base and on who is batting (p00:5E74, p00:5E7A): eight tunes, plus the seventh inning ones. The home run is two requests in a row: 0x2C (PSG and one SCC channel) and 0x3F (the other three SCC channels). F5 turns the music off and on.

The pieces have not been rewritten: the cartridge's own engine runs on a Python Z80 (tools/musica.py) and what it writes to the PSG and the SCC is turned into sound. Each one has been checked against openMSX (tools/omsx_psg.tcl, tools/coteja_psg.py): the state of both chips matches frame by frame. Seven match with the "free tone" of channel 4 switched off (0xEFF4): a whistle the game turns on by itself during a game, which those pieces do not use. The effects are not here.
