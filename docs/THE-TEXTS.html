The texts

100 texts taken from the ROM with tools/textos.py. The text routines at p00:4EA6 read a position [x y] and then font characters up to 0xFF; 0xFE brings another position and 0xFD is a half space (p00:4EC0). The list has the addresses the code loads just before calling them, those of their pointer tables and those of the texts drawn one after another.

The characters come from the game's font (tools/fuente.py): digits, letters, hiragana, katakana and kanji. The kanji of the PENNANT screens (試合結果, 順位表...) use codes that are other characters during a game: another kanji set is loaded on top (p00:52D9, p00:52C7). The ● is character 0x9A, the marker of the chosen option (p01:65AF).

The position is in screen pixels. "Where" is the address of the text and "drawn from" the instruction that asks for it.
