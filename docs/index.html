What turned up when we took it apart

It saves to the Game Master 2's SRAM

Besides disk, TEAM EDIT and PENNANT save to Konami's Game Master 2: it finds it by the YZ mark at 0x4010, puts its bank 4 at 0x8000 and calls its SRAM routine with the command in C (p13:709A, p13:721D). In the Game Master 2 disassembly that routine is p04:8000, the door to its file system.

Read in the code.

The speed, in real km/h

The three SPD digits on the pitcher's card give the speed of the ball: 80 to 199 km/h (p03:AAE1). Pressing down on release makes it a quarter faster and up a quarter slower; with no stamina left it loses 4 to 7 km/h (p03:ABAA).

Read in the code.

The ten-run rule

After any bottom half, a ten-run lead ends the game (p02:9424). From the ninth on the usual rule applies: it ends if the home team is ahead or if the score is not tied when the inning closes (p02:9428).

Read in the code.

PENNANT draws lots for the games you do not play

With a single player the other two games of the round are not played: each team gets 0 to 15 runs at random and, on a tie, one of the two gets one more (p15:AC31).

Read in the code.

The home run is two tunes at once

The game asks for 0x2C, on the PSG and one SCC channel, and right after for 0x3F, which plays the other three SCC channels (p02:9357).

Checked against openMSX.

The music follows the runners

While a team bats, the background tune depends on the furthest occupied base and on who is batting: four tunes for the visitors and four for the home team (p00:5E74).

Checked against openMSX.
