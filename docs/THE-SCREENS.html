The screens

All of them are drawn with our own code (tools/vram.py and tools/pantallas.py) following the steps of the routine that paints them, cited for each one. None is a capture. Each one has been compared with the openMSX VRAM dumped with the game running (tools/omsx_vram.tcl, tools/coteja_vram.py); under each one is how much matches.

The game runs in SCREEN 5 on the V9938 (256 × 212 pixels, 16 colours). Almost everything is drawn in two steps: first the graphics are built on page 1 of the VRAM (the "sheet", 32 characters per row) and then copied to the visible page with VDP commands.

The intro

The "THEプロ野球 激突ペナントレース" title (p15:BB97, p00:52E7): the pictures at p09:B07A and the character list at p09:B6B9, built on page 1. The intro uncovers it column by column (p15:BC39). Checked: 0 bytes different.

The stadium below it (p00:5427): the pictures at p09:A619 and 16 × 11 characters in runs from p09:AFC4, with the palette at p07:6000. Checked: 0 bytes different.

The field

The field has three zones: the centre and the two sides. Each one is 256 × 512 pixels and is a map of 16 × 8 tiles of 4 × 4 characters (p08:9432); the tiles come from p08:95B8 (and from p08:9A58 in the last two strips of the list). The screen shows part of the zone at a time. Checked: centre zone, the 256 lines that were in the VRAM, identical in two dumps; in another three, 8 to 22 lines differ: the signs and strips the game was drawing at that moment.

The left field zone (tiles at p08:9B28).

The right field zone (tiles at p09:A088). These two are drawn with the same code as the centre one, but they are not checked: the demo never leaves the centre zone.

The pitch

The pitching screen, seen from behind home plate (p01:6352): the background in runs from p08:9220, two frames and the text at p13:7E1E (S B O, the 回 of the inning, HR and 打率). With no game going, the boxes and figures are empty. Checked: 728 of 864 blocks identical; the rest is the little bases window and the figures of the game being played.

The sprites

The 64 patterns of 16 × 16 used in a game (VRAM 0xF800-0xFFFF), set by p00:537C, p00:539D and p00:53CC. During a play p00:572E changes the pitcher's pose at 0xFAC0 and p00:5716 the catcher's at 0xFFC0. Checked: identical except for those two poses, which depend on the moment.

The font

161 one-bit characters at p12:B6C3: digits, letters, hiragana, katakana and signs. They are put twice on the sheet, the second time in colour 4. On top go two kanji sets: 17 from p09:B942 for the game (戦打数安本塁三振四死球盗失策防御率) and 11 from p09:B9DA for the PENNANT screens (試合結果勝敗差順位表 and a quote mark).

The Pro Yakyuu Gekitotsu Pennant Race was published by Konami for the MSX2 in 1988; its catalogue number is RC-757 and it is 128 KB with the SCC. All rights remain with their holders. This is preservation, study and documentation work, and the cartridge image is not distributed.
